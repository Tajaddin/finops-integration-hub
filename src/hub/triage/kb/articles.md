# Ledgerline help centre: billing and account articles

These are the knowledge-base articles the triage agent retrieves and cites. Each starts with its id. They describe
the fictional company in this project (seat-based software, priced in USD, EUR and GBP, invoiced from Odoo).

## kb-001 Refund policy
Annual plans can be refunded pro rata within 30 days of the invoice date; after 30 days annual plans are not
refundable, but unused seats can be removed at renewal. Monthly plans can be refunded in full within 7 days of the
invoice date, not after. Refunds are paid to the original payment method. Any refund above 500 in the invoice's
currency is reviewed and approved by the finance team before it is paid; support staff cannot promise one.

## kb-002 Getting a copy of an invoice
Every invoice is available as a PDF in the customer portal under Billing, then Invoices. Invoice numbers look like
INV/2026/00123. Support can e-mail a copy to the billing contact on the account; we never send invoices to an
address that is not on the account.

## kb-003 Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards, ACH debit for USD accounts, SEPA Direct Debit for EUR
accounts, BACS for GBP accounts, and bank wire for Enterprise plans. We do not accept cheques or cryptocurrency.

## kb-004 Failed or declined payments
When a card payment fails we retry on days 1, 3 and 7 and e-mail the billing contact a link to update the card.
An invoice that is 30 days overdue suspends access until it is paid. Payment problems are handled by the
Collections team, who can set up a payment plan.

## kb-005 Payment terms and due dates
Invoices are due 30 days after the invoice date (Net 30); the due date is printed on the invoice. We charge no
late fees. Reminders go out 7, 14 and 30 days after the due date.

## kb-006 Cancellation and cancellation fees
Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and stop at the end of the paid month, with no fee. Annual plans
cancelled within 30 days of the invoice are refunded pro rata (see kb-001); after that, an early-termination fee of
25% of the remaining contract value applies.

## kb-007 Tracking a refund
An approved refund reaches cards in 5 to 10 business days and bank accounts in 3 to 5. It appears on statements
as LEDGERLINE REFUND with the invoice number. Support can confirm whether a refund was approved and when it was
paid.

## kb-008 Changing billing details
The billing contact, billing address and tax ID can be changed in the portal under Billing, then Details, by an
account administrator. Changes apply to future invoices; to reissue an existing invoice with new details, ask
support with the invoice number.

## kb-009 Currencies and price lists
Prices are set per currency (USD, EUR, GBP); we do not convert between them. An account's currency is fixed at
signup. Price changes are announced 30 days before they apply at renewal.

## kb-010 Reaching support or a person
Support answers by e-mail at support@ledgerline.example and by chat in the app, Monday to Friday, 8 am to 8 pm
Eastern. Ask for a human at any time and the conversation is handed to an agent. Billing emergencies (a suspended
account) can be raised as urgent.

## kb-011 Accounts, seats and administrators
An account administrator can add or remove seats, invite users and change roles in the portal. Added seats are
billed pro rata for the rest of the term. Deleting an account is permanent after 30 days and requires an
administrator to confirm by e-mail.

## kb-012 Disputes and chargebacks
If you do not recognise a charge, contact us before disputing it with your bank: most are a second invoice for
added seats. A chargeback suspends the account until it is resolved.
